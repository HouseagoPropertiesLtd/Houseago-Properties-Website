Privacy policy

Houseago Properties Ltd ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who visits this website, enquires about a home, rents from us, or asks us to manage their property. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and the choices and rights you have over it.

Who we are

Houseago Properties Ltd is a student housing and property management company based at The Enterprise Centre, University of East Anglia, Norwich, NR4 7TJ. For the purposes of data protection law, we are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us using the details at the bottom of this page.

The data we collect

What we collect depends on how you interact with us. It generally falls into the following groups.

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, and the content of any message you send us through the contact form or by email.
  • Tenancy and portal accounts: your name, email address, tenancy details, and the documents associated with your tenancy, such as your tenancy agreement, right to rent check, inventory, and safety certificates.
  • Referencing and right to rent: identity and referencing information we are required to collect and verify before a tenancy begins, which may include proof of identity, immigration status where relevant, and references from previous landlords or employers.
  • Rent and deposits: payment and bank details needed to collect rent and to register a deposit with a government approved scheme.
  • Landlord instructions: contact details and property information provided by landlords who instruct us to manage their property.
  • Website usage: basic technical information such as browser type and pages visited. We do not currently use non-essential cookies, advertising trackers, or third party analytics on this website.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

We use personal data to respond to enquiries, to set up and manage tenancies, to manage properties on behalf of landlords, to collect rent and protect deposits, to carry out the right to rent and referencing checks we are legally required to perform, to keep the student portal secure, and to meet our obligations to bodies such as the National Residential Landlords Association and the Deposit Protection Service.

Depending on the activity, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases: performance of a tenancy or management contract, compliance with a legal obligation (such as right to rent checks), our legitimate interests in running a safe and well managed letting business, and, where relevant, your consent.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only where necessary, with organisations such as:

  • The Deposit Protection Service, to register and protect tenancy deposits
  • Referencing and right to rent checking providers, to verify prospective tenants before a tenancy begins
  • Trusted trade professionals and contractors, where needed to carry out maintenance you have reported
  • Supabase, our student portal and document storage provider, which processes data on our behalf as part of running the portal securely
  • Landlords, in relation to the property they own and the tenancy connected to it
  • Regulators, local authorities, or law enforcement, where we are legally required to share information

Some of our service providers, including Supabase, may process data on servers outside the United Kingdom. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, to keep your data protected to a UK standard.

How long we keep it

We keep personal data for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, and afterwards for as long as we are required to by law, for example to meet tax, accounting, or deposit protection obligations. Enquiry details that do not lead to a tenancy are kept only for as long as reasonably needed to handle that enquiry, and are then deleted.

Keeping your data secure

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse, or unauthorised access, including access controls on the student portal and secure storage of tenancy documents. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to keep risk as low as reasonably possible.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Ask us what personal data we hold about you, and get a copy of it
  • Ask us to correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Ask us to delete your data, where we are not required to keep it
  • Ask us to restrict or object to certain uses of your data
  • Ask us to provide your data in a portable format, where relevant
  • Withdraw consent, where we rely on consent, at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Our services are aimed at university students and landlords, and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we change how the website or portal works. The version on this page is always the current one.
