For landlords

Management services you can trust

Three packages, built specifically for student properties, from a one-off tenant find through to fully comprehensive management. Every managed property receives a pre-management inspection before we take it on, and VAT does not apply to our fees.

Management packages

Tenant find, moderate, or comprehensive - the choice is yours

Tenant find

A one-off service to find the right tenants for your property.

  • Online advertising across all major portals
  • Accompanied viewings
  • Professional tenant referencing
  • Right to Rent checks
  • Tenancy agreement preparation
  • Arrange initial safety certificates

Moderate management

Setup and ongoing fees tailored to your property.

  • Everything included in Tenant find
  • Pre-management inspection before we take on the property
  • Rent paid directly to you - we do not hold client funds
  • Damage deposit protected through the Deposit Protection Service
  • Tenancy renewal service - add-on, available on request
  • End of tenancy handover, inventory not included

Comprehensive management

Setup and ongoing fees tailored to your property, reflecting the fuller scope of cover.

  • Everything included in Moderate management
  • Guarantor contract organisation
  • Tenant verification and background checks
  • Full maintenance handling, start to finish, using trusted trade professionals
  • Regular property inspections
  • Check in and check out inventory, with photographs and condition reports by email
  • Deposit negotiation at the end of tenancy
