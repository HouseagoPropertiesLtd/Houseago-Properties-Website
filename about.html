About us

About Houseago Properties Ltd

Houseago Properties Ltd was built on a simple idea: student housing should be run by people who understand what it is actually like to be a student.

Our approach

A student's perspective on property management

Our team brings hands-on property management experience together with a genuine student's point of view. That combination shapes everything Houseago Properties Ltd does, from how homes are maintained to how quickly the phone gets answered.

Students deserve a safe, comfortable home, managed by people who genuinely care about them.

The mission

Two missions, one standard

Mission one

Balancing competitive pricing with luxury

We deliver competitively priced, high quality homes and back that up with responsive maintenance, open communication, and a genuinely trustworthy relationship between management and students.

Mission two

First class property management

For landlords, that means real experience in a complex market, straightforward professionalism, and integrity you can trust.
