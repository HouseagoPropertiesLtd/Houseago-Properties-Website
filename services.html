FAQs

Common questions

What deposits do I need to pay?

A holding deposit and a damage deposit, both set per student and protected through the Deposit Protection Service. Get in touch and we will confirm the figures for your property.

Do the utility packages cover everything?

Silver covers electricity, gas, and water. Gold adds wifi and the TV licence on top.

What comes with the property already?

Every home includes a double bed, study desk, wardrobe space, TV, washing machine, dryer, dishwasher, microwave, toaster, and kettle.

Who do I contact if something needs fixing?

You will have a direct line to the management team, and the student portal, for reporting maintenance issues quickly.
